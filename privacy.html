Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy describes how data is handled by Vantage Studio's applications, including Hermes, a personal productivity assistant.

What we access

With the user's explicit permission, Hermes accesses the user's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) solely to provide personal productivity features such as reading and organising the user's email, summarising it, and drafting replies on the user's behalf.

What we do with it

  • Accessed data is processed for the user's own use only and is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  • No Google data is used for advertising, and no human review of the data is performed.
  • Data is stored on the user's own private infrastructure.

Control

The user may revoke access at any time via their Google Account security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or by contacting us.
